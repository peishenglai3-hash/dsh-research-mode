# Research design

Use when a project needs a defensible empirical design before data collection.

## Contract

1. State the question in one sentence. It must be answerable with evidence, not only with a theoretical stance.
2. Name the mechanism or comparison; distinguish description, association and causal claim.
3. Specify data: source, unit, period, exclusions, and what is not covered.
4. Specify the comparison or counterfactual. If none, do not write causal language.
5. State what evidence would count against the claim.
6. Record the design as a project artifact before execution.

## Do not

- Do not turn a literature gap into a data claim without checking coverage.
- Do not choose a method because it is fashionable; choose the weakest design that can answer the question.
- Do not hide access limits, sample quotas or missing variables.
